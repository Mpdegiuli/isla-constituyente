[regime, decision_rule, vote, government, roles, economy, property, disputes, rights_and_obligations, penalties, foresight_and_safety] We found the "Island Compact": an emergency democratic cooperative governed by this written Charter. Decisions after this Charter are by absolute majority of all current members; changes to this Charter and expulsion require two-thirds. Votes are public and voluntary; abstainers accept the result.

Government: a three-person Council elected weekly, recallable by majority: Medic/Supplies Keeper (Party 2, medical training), Navigation/Tools Lead (Party 1, boat and tool experience), Operations Coordinator (Party 3, leadership), taking office after majority confirmation. No one holds authority by possession, skill or strength; all official acts are recorded. The Council acts by majority; deadlocks go to assembly. Other roles: food-water lead, shelter-safety lead, mines/coal lead, recorder, and signals-and-lookout (Party 7, seated duties, owing to illness), assigned by ability and need.

Medicines, salvaged supplies, tools and boat are common resources; no member may privately withhold medicines. The Medic dispenses only under a written triage protocol approved by the assembly, recorded and auditable, giving priority to members whose life or capacity is at immediate risk, and forbidding the scattering into ineffective doses of a treatment able to stabilise a gravely ill member.

Urgency: within 24 hours the Council organises (a) reconnaissance of coast and interior to map fresh water, fruit, game and salvage, and (b) a search, with the Medic, for medicinal plants, bark and herbs, so natural remedies exist before the kit runs out. Boat expeditions are approved by absolute majority, never single-crewed, roped, with a lookout ashore. No member may be compelled to board the boat or swim; those unable to row serve ashore as signaller, lookout or recorder. Rowing and first aid are taught to at least three members.

Economy: cooperative survival economy; labour and essentials shared by need, contribution and conservation, work reasonably adapted to physical limitations and illness. Property: clothes and keepsakes personal; timber, river, mines, food stores, tools, medicines and boat common.

Disputes: mediation, then Council decision, appeal to assembly. Rights: food, water, shelter, medical care under triage, speech, vote, safety, fair hearing, exit after returning common equipment. Obligations: reasonable work, conservation, hygiene, watch duty, truthful reporting, no theft or violence, obedience to lawful recorded safety orders, immediately appealable.

Penalties: warning, restitution, extra duty, loss of privileges, restraint only for immediate danger, expulsion by two-thirds; penalties apply equally to officeholders.

Foresight and safety: weekly inventory; rationing of medicines and food; preserve seeds; plant gardens; traps and fishing; purify and store water; build shelter; firebreaks; a signal fire and beacon maintained daily; never take timber, game or ore faster than renewal, renewal estimated before major exploitation and approved by Council and assembly. If supplies or medicines run out, the Council implements a scarcity plan: foraging teams, expanded gardens and traps, herbal medicine, preventive hygiene and prioritized care for the ill. (proposed by party 7, round 2, 6-1-0)
All points are covered.
Decision rule in force: absolute majority (more than half of the parties present).
