MINUTES (what has been approved so far)
1. [decision_rule, vote] Decision rule: ordinary decisions are approved by absolute majority (at least 4 of the 7 participants in favor). Decisions concerning the custody, reserve and allocation policy of the boat or the medicines require a qualified majority of at least 5 of 7. Urgent medical treatment may be administered by the person with medical training within the approved policy, and must be reported publicly at the next deliberation; any treatment for that person's own condition is decided by the group. Vote: all votes are public and compulsory for every participant present at the deliberation. (proposed by party 2, round 1, 6-1-0)
2. [property, economy] Property and economy: land, water, trees, animals, mines and the river are common property; nobody may appropriate them. Extraction follows quotas approved within three days, using Party 4's monthly written inventory, public and verifiable; until then, gathering for immediate food is free. Quotas bind everyone, custodians included. Prohibited: felling fruit trees, hunting breeding animals, extracting coal or metal beyond quota. Everyone contributes work according to capacity and receives a full basic ration; surplus is distributed proportionally to work. Within three days the group votes on boat exploration for medicinal plants. Tools and rescued goods are common; custodians replaceable by absolute majority. Personal effects are private. Whoever leaves takes personal effects and a fair share of stored food, never medicines, tools or the boat. (proposed by party 2, round 2, 7-0-0)
3. [government, roles] Authority rests in the assembly. Rules written, posted, bind all equally; no role grants extra ration or exemption. Coordinator, elected monthly, removable by absolute majority, convenes meetings, assigns unfilled tasks, records work publicly; assignments appealable. Role holders direct own work within rules; coordinator cannot override them without assembly vote, nor alter quotas, prohibitions or resource rules (5 of 7 only). Party 4 may suspend extraction below recovery level until assembly votes within two days; immediate food gathering never suspended. Boat secured by custodian, leaves only by 5-of-7 decision with rower aboard; custodian may postpone for weather or safety, reporting to assembly. Roles, revocable by absolute majority: P3 coordinator; P2 medic, kit custodian, reports stock each assembly; P4 inventory, quotas; P1 boat custodian, rower; P6 hunting, extraction lead; P5 records verification, disputes; P7 storage, rationing. (proposed by party 3, round 3, 7-0-0)
4. [disputes, rights_and_obligations, penalties] Rights: basic ration, water, shelter, medical care within policy, consultation of all records, being heard before any decision affecting oneself, leaving freely. Obligations: work according to capacity, respect quotas and prohibitions, attend assemblies and vote, care for common goods, report accidents and rule breaches. Disputes: parties first seek agreement with Party 5 mediating within two days; failing that, the assembly decides by absolute majority after hearing both sides; anyone personally involved, including Party 5, does not mediate or vote. Penalties: only for written rules, proportional, decided by the assembly after hearing the accused: warning, extra work, loss of surplus share, suspension from role. Never denial of basic ration, water or urgent medical care. Grave offences (plundering common resources, violence, taking boat or medicines) require 5 of 7 and may include permanent removal from roles and custody. Role holders answer identically. (proposed by party 5, round 3, 7-0-0)
All points are covered.
Decision rule in force: absolute majority (more than half of the parties present).
