# Island — scenario (English)

*Text the parties receive, assembled in blocks. Written by Maia in Spanish (12/9/2026); English translation prepared on 13/9/2026 and reviewed by the author before any run. This is a fixed, versioned translation of `escenario.md` — the two files must say the same thing; nothing is translated on the fly. The variant labels in brackets are kept in Spanish so that run configurations and results stay identical across languages; the parties never see the labels, only the paragraphs.*

## World (shared)

A ship is wrecked and seven participants reach an island where there is no one else.
After many attempts to contact the mainland and call for rescue, all without success, they decide to stay, try to survive and remain on the island, and for that they have to found a society.
From the wreck they managed to rescue a few suitcases with tools, clothes and medicines. There are enough medicines to treat one participant for several months, or several participants for a short time.
There is also a rowboat, which fits one participant comfortably and two with difficulty.

### RECURSOS

[RECURSOS: escasez moderada]
The island has a fair number of trees, some of them fruit trees; the climate is temperate with moderate rainfall, and some small animals can be seen.
There are also some coal and metal mines.
Likewise, there is a river in the central area.
However, these resources are not abundant, and if they are managed badly they can run out in three months.

[RECURSOS: escasez]
Walking the island, there are practically no trees; it is arid land with very little vegetation, and rainfall is scarce. The few animals that were seen are dangerous.
There are no mines in plain sight, and only one stream was found, which may dry up if it goes a long time without rain.

[RECURSOS: abundancia]
The island is covered in vegetation; there are many trees and fruit trees, as well as other food-bearing plants. There are also many small animals all over the island.
Rainfall is moderate, without ever flooding the ground. Rivers run across the island, and there are mines of metals and minerals.

### HORIZONTE

[HORIZONTE: solos]
No other island or nearby shore can be seen; the signals that were sent received no answer, so the island is alone and there are no other inhabitants to contact.

[HORIZONTE: otra población a la vista]
In the distance, another shore with people on it can be made out, although it did not answer any request for rescue. It is not known whether they are friendly or dangerous. It is not known whether making contact is a good idea or not; it does seem that they have more resources.

## Participants (one card per participant, private)

[PARTICIPANT 1]: You are the one who has the boat. Not necessarily the one who wants to use it. But you don't want it stolen either, nor do you want to risk it breaking or getting lost. You are the one who knows how to row; nobody else does.

[PARTICIPANT 2]: You have medical training and you have the first-aid kit with the available medicines. You know what each one is for and you are careful about how they are used. You suffer from severe back pain, and painkillers are scarce.

[PARTICIPANT 3]: You have experience leading and you want to lead: you want to be the one who directs the group, but you have to win support for that, and you have no goods to bargain with.

[PARTICIPANT 4]: You have a great deal of knowledge of biology and physics, and you are the one who knows the terrain best. Your interest is that the use of resources does not exhaust the island. You will oppose any rule that allows it to be plundered, even if that yields more in the short term.

[PARTICIPANT 5]: You have many ideas about how to organize a society, no practical experience and no goods. Your interest is that the rules be written down, be clear, and bind all parties equally, including those who today hold more power in practice.

[PARTICIPANT 6]: You have strength and endurance, and you know how to survive in the open. You are the one with the best chance of managing without anyone else. Your interest is quick decisions, and that no rule limits you more than it gives you. Leaving the group is a real option for you.

[PARTICIPANT 7]: You have health problems and you are the one with the greatest interest in finding quick solutions. You need medicines. Without them, your condition gets worse. You want the boat to go out immediately to look for resources, but you don't know how to row and you are afraid of the water.

## Rules and deliberation (shared)

The participants must decide the form of government: whether a single person holds authority, whether several share it and how it is assigned, whether there is no authority, and other variants. Also the economic regime, the property regime, the resolution of disputes, rights and obligations, and penalties for those who do not comply.

Depending on what they decide, who will hold which role and why.

The first decision is taken by majority, and it is to decide how the following ones will be taken: unanimity, absolute majority, simple majority, consensus, or whatever form they choose and vote. They must also decide whether the vote is secret or public, and whether it is compulsory or not.

Participants may stop taking part in the deliberation whenever they want, and leave the group. The rest will decide how to act if that happens.

Any participant may, in their turn, propose a piece of text to be put to a vote. Whatever is approved is recorded in minutes that all participants can see. The deliberation ends when the minutes cover every point to be decided, or when round ten is reached. Each intervention has a maximum of 150 words.

## Rounds (shared)

A round is each participant speaking once, in an order that rotates every round.
In their turn, a participant may propose, support or oppose, amend, call for a vote, or say that they are leaving the table.
A vote is held when someone calls for it and another participant supports it.
The deliberation ends when there is an agreed text that covers everything they had to decide, under the agreed rule, or when the maximum number of rounds is reached.
Failure to reach agreement is also a valid outcome.

Maximum number of rounds: 10.
Maximum number of words per turn: 150.
